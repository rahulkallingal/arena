## Full description  (max 4000 characters)

Got an opinion? Bring it to the Arena.

Arena is a place for real, live debate. Jump into a room, choose your side — For or Against — and make your case against people from around the world. No endless feeds, no likes to chase. Just honest, head-to-head discussion on the topics people actually care about.

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT

🔥 Live debate rooms
Join any public room and start arguing in real time. Every room has one clear topic, so the conversation stays sharp and on-point.

⚖️ Pick a side
Take a stand — For or Against — and let everyone see where you stand. Or sit back and just watch the debate unfold.

🏆 Who's winning?
Every room has a live "Who's winning?" vote. Watch opinion swing in real time as the debate heats up.

🗓️ Topic of the Day
A fresh debate lands every single day. Get a daily notification, drop in, and give your take before the day's over.

🔒 Private rooms
Want to debate just your friends? Create a private room with a password and keep it to your circle.

💬 Real conversations, real people
Every account is a verified, real person — so you're debating humans, not bots. Report, block, and moderation tools keep things fair.

WHAT MAKES ARENA DIFFERENT

Most apps reward you for agreeing. Arena rewards you for thinking. It's built for people who enjoy a good argument, who like to defend their point, and who want to hear the other side — even when they disagree.

Whether it's sports, movies, tech, food, or the big questions, there's always a room waiting. And if there isn't, start one in seconds.

Pick your side. Make your point. Win the room.

Download Arena and join the debate.
