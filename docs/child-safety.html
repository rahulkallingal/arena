Arena — Child Safety Standards

Last updated: July 2026

Arena is committed to keeping its community safe and has a zero-tolerance approach to child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). This page explains our standards and how we act on them.

1. Arena is an adults-only service

Arena is intended for users aged 18 and older. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly allow anyone under 18 to create an account. Users confirm they are 18 or older when they sign up.

2. Zero tolerance for child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and exploitation

We strictly prohibit any content or behaviour that sexualises, exploits, or endangers minors, including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, or attempts to solicit or contact minors. Accounts involved in such activity are permanently banned.

3. How users can report

  • Any message or user can be reported from inside the app.
  • Users can block other users.
  • Child-safety concerns can also be emailed directly to cryptork97@gmail.com.

4. How we respond

Reports are reviewed promptly. When CSAE is found we remove the content, permanently ban the account, and, where required by law, report it to the relevant authorities (such as the NCMEC or local law enforcement) and preserve evidence for them.

5. Legal compliance

We comply with applicable child-safety laws and cooperate with law enforcement and recognised child-protection organisations.

6. Child safety point of contact

For any child-safety question or report, contact our designated point of contact at cryptork97@gmail.com.
